# Family Insurance Summary

## Health Insurance

The family floater health insurance policy is HX-221 with Suraksha General Insurance. It covers four members: Ravi, Meera, Anaya and Kabir. The sum insured is 10 lakh rupees with a no-claim bonus of 2 lakh rupees.

The policy renews on 10 December 2026. The annual premium is 18,400 rupees, paid by bank transfer from the joint savings account.

Pre-existing conditions are covered after a waiting period of 36 months. Maternity cover is not included.

## Car Insurance

The car insurance policy CI-7781 covers the 2021 Hyundai Creta. It is a comprehensive policy with zero depreciation and roadside assistance add-ons.

The car insurance renews on 3 January 2027. The insured declared value is 9.2 lakh rupees.

## Term Life Insurance

Ravi holds a term life insurance policy TL-55012 with a cover of 1.5 crore rupees. The policy term ends in 2054 and Meera is the nominee.
